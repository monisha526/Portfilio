# SIH 2026 — Problem Statement Expansion
## Problem statements 229 → 240

### Background
Smart India Hackathon 2026 has expanded the available problem-statement range from 229 to 240. The update represents **11 new problem statements** now available through the SIH portal, giving student teams more innovation challenge opportunities to explore.

The expanded range covered by this update is:

| Range | Portal update |
